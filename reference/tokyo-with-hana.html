Irasshaimase! I'm Mori Hana, your travel assistant. For the next three days we travel Tokyo the honest way: every day starts at Tokyo Station, we ride real trains, and we walk the last stretch on foot.

Each ride has a train video so you can sit in the front car with me. Each sight has a Google Maps walking route and a free Street View link, so you can look around as if you were standing there.

Otsukaresama deshita! Thank you for travelling with me. Street View images are taken on different days, so sometimes the weather changes between two clicks. That is part of the fun.

How the links work: “Walk in Street View” opens Google Maps walking directions and drops you into Street View where the walk begins, usually the station exit, facing the way to go. The route stays drawn on the small map in the corner, so you can click forward along it. Close Street View to see the full directions list. “Ride video” opens a YouTube search for front-view (前面展望) recordings of that exact stretch, so you can pick a current, high-quality ride. Travel times are typical daytime values.
